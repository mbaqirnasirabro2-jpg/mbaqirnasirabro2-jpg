### Featured Portfolio Project

#### **Clinical Health Risk Prediction (`clinical-health-risk-prediction-ml`)**
* **Focus:** Imbalanced clinical risk screening (~1.94% positive prevalence) using 5-fold Stratified Out-of-Fold (OOF) validation.
* **Key Experiments:** Evaluated 14-feature baseline (`ROC-AUC: 0.8092`, `PR-AUC: 0.1247`, `LogLoss: 0.0851`), feature ablation (11 features), `scale_pos_weight`, and Sigmoid probability calibration (`LogLoss: 0.0869`, `Brier: 0.0186`, reaching `0.8824` recall at a `0.01` screening threshold).
* **Repo & Wiki:** [Project Repository](https://github.com/mbaqirnasirabro2-jpg/clinical-health-risk-prediction-ml) • [Experiments Wiki](https://github.com/mbaqirnasirabro2-jpg/clinical-health-risk-prediction-ml/wiki/Experiments)
